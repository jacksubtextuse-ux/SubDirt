# Fee Taxonomy — Required Categories, Aliases, and Where to Find Them

Every category below must be **explicitly addressed** in the master fee table, even when the verified
answer is $0, N/A, or "not levied here." A category you silently omit is indistinguishable from a
category you forgot to check — and the whole point of this skill is that nothing was forgotten.

For each category, the **Search-as** line lists alias terms. Agencies rename fees constantly and the
same charge appears under different labels across documents, so run each alias as its own search;
treating synonyms as one search is how renamed fees get missed. Combine an alias with the jurisdiction
name and the word "fee schedule," "rate," or "resolution" (e.g. `"<city> transportation impact fee
schedule"`, `"<utility> capacity charge rate sheet"`).

The **Look-in** line points at the primary-source document type most likely to hold the verified number.

---

## 1. Permitting

Charges to review and inspect the work itself.

- **Subtypes:** building permit, grading/earthwork permit, site-development permit, demolition permit,
  plan review/plan check, inspection, re-inspection, resubmittal/recheck.
- **Search-as:** building permit fee, plan check fee, plan review fee, grading permit, site work
  permit, land disturbance permit, demolition permit, inspection fee, re-inspection fee, resubmittal
  fee, technology/automation surcharge, records/archive fee, SMIP/strong-motion (CA), green-building
  surcharge.
- **Look-in:** the building department's master/consolidated fee schedule; many permit fees are valuation-based
  tables (e.g. ICC/UBC-derived). Capture the *table and method*, not a single point estimate.

## 2. Impact / Infrastructure

One-time charges to fund the capacity your project consumes.

- **Subtypes:** transportation/road/mobility, traffic concurrency, general infrastructure, capital
  facilities, system development charges.
- **Search-as:** transportation impact fee, road impact fee, mobility fee, traffic impact fee,
  concurrency fee, multimodal fee, capital facilities fee, infrastructure fee, system development
  charge (SDC), development impact fee (DIF), AB1600/Mitigation Fee Act report (CA), proportionate-share
  payment (FL).
- **Look-in:** the adopted impact-fee study / nexus study and the annually adopted fee schedule that
  implements it. Impact fees almost always have a per-unit, per-bed, or per-sf basis and are zoned —
  confirm which fee district the address sits in.

## 3. Utilities

Connection and capacity charges from water/sewer/stormwater providers — frequently **independent
authorities**, not the city.

- **Subtypes:** water connection, sewer connection, stormwater, meter, tap, capacity, availability,
  backflow, grease interceptor.
- **Search-as:** water connection fee, sewer connection fee, tap fee, meter fee/installation,
  capacity fee, capacity reservation charge, system development charge, availability fee, plant
  investment fee (PIF), facility investment fee, will-serve/will-serve letter fee, backflow prevention
  fee, grease interceptor/FOG fee, stormwater connection/SDC, stormwater utility fee.
- **Look-in:** each utility authority's own rate sheet / rules-and-regulations / fee resolution.
  Water and sewer are often *different* authorities from each other and from the city. Capacity fees
  are usually sized by meter size or fixture-unit count — capture the sizing basis.

## 4. Education

School-system charges tied to the residents the project generates.

- **Search-as:** school impact fee, school facilities fee, education facilities fee, student
  generation fee, developer fee (CA Level 1/2/3, Ed. Code §17620), capital facilities charge (school).
- **Look-in:** the school district's adopted developer-fee justification/study and fee resolution.
  **Note the student-housing nuance:** student-generation methodologies often assume family households;
  whether a fee applies to non-family student housing, and at what rate, can turn on the unit/bedroom
  definition — capture the reasoning, don't assume exemption.

## 5. Parks / Open Space

- **Search-as:** park impact fee, parks & recreation fee, open-space fee, parkland dedication fee,
  in-lieu (fee-in-lieu of dedication), Quimby Act fee (CA), recreation facilities fee.
- **Look-in:** parks impact-fee study and the subdivision/SALDO ordinance for dedication-vs-in-lieu
  formulas.

## 6. Fire / EMS

- **Search-as:** fire impact fee, fire facilities fee, EMS impact fee, fire plan review, fire
  sprinkler/alarm permit, fire flow / hydrant fee, fire-district assessment.
- **Look-in:** the fire-protection district's fee schedule (may be a separate district from the city)
  plus the building department for fire plan review/permit.

## 7. Police / Public Safety

- **Search-as:** police impact fee, public safety impact fee, law enforcement facilities fee.
- **Look-in:** public-safety impact-fee study; often bundled into a combined "public facilities" fee.

## 8. Environmental

- **Subtypes:** tree mitigation, wetland, watershed, water quality, erosion/sediment control, habitat.
- **Search-as:** tree mitigation/removal fee, tree preservation in-lieu, landscape/canopy fee, wetland
  mitigation, water-quality/NPDES fee, MS4 fee, erosion & sediment control permit, land-disturbance
  fee, habitat conservation fee (e.g. HCP/MSHCP regional fees), stormwater quality fee.
- **Look-in:** environmental/engineering standards, the stormwater/watershed authority, and any
  regional habitat-conservation plan with a per-acre fee.

## 9. Affordable Housing / Inclusionary

- **Search-as:** inclusionary housing fee, affordable housing in-lieu fee, housing impact/linkage fee,
  commercial linkage fee, jobs-housing fee, density-bonus processing.
- **Look-in:** the inclusionary/affordable-housing ordinance. Capture whether student housing is
  exempt, subject, or eligible for a density-bonus tradeoff — this is frequently contested for PBSH.

## 10. Special-District Assessments

- **Search-as:** CFD/Mello-Roos (CA), CDD assessment (FL), special assessment district, business
  improvement district (BID), downtown improvement district, metro/metropolitan district (CO),
  benefit assessment, PID (TX) levy.
- **Look-in:** the district's formation documents / rate-and-method-of-apportionment and the county
  assessor or district administrator. **These are recurring** — flag annual vs. one-time.

## 11. Professional / Consultant Review

- **Search-as:** third-party plan review, outside consultant review deposit, engineering review
  deposit, peer-review fee, cost-recovery/actual-cost billing, deposit-based review account.
- **Look-in:** development manual / application packet; often a **deposit against actual cost**, not a
  fixed fee — capture the deposit amount and the billing mechanism.

## 12. Bonds / Securities / Letters of Credit

Not a fee, but a capital requirement — capture the amount/basis and the instrument.

- **Search-as:** performance bond, maintenance bond, subdivision improvement security, erosion-control
  bond, right-of-way/encroachment bond, landscape maintenance security, letter of credit requirement.
- **Look-in:** subdivision/engineering standards and the development agreement template.

## 13. Recording / Platting / Subdivision

- **Search-as:** plat/final-map recording fee, subdivision application fee, lot consolidation, parcel
  map, easement recording, mylar fee, county recorder fee.
- **Look-in:** the subdivision ordinance and the county recorder's fee schedule.

## 14. State-Level Fees (if applicable)

- **Search-as:** state plan review/HCD, state fire marshal review, CEQA/NEPA processing, SWPPP/NOI
  (state stormwater permit), state water-resources permit, energy-code/Title-24 (CA), accessibility/CASp,
  state environmental review fee.
- **Look-in:** the relevant state agency's fee schedule. Applicability varies sharply by state — verify
  rather than assume either presence or absence.

## 15. Recurring Annual Charges

Anything that recurs after entitlement/permit — flag clearly so it lands in operating, not development, cost.

- **Search-as:** stormwater utility fee, special-district annual assessment (CFD/CDD/PID/metro),
  fire-district tax, BID assessment, annual backflow test fee, annual fire-inspection fee.
- **Look-in:** the same instruments as the one-time charges; the distinction is *frequency*, which the
  master table's "timing" column must make explicit.

---

## Completeness discipline

Before declaring Step 3 done, walk this list top to bottom and confirm each category has at least one
row — even if that row says "$0 — not levied; <citation>." If a category truly has no analog in this
jurisdiction, say so with the citation that establishes it. The goal is that a reader can see you
*checked* every category, not merely that you *listed* the ones that happened to apply.
